# Meeting Room Booking Procedures

Our office facilities include several meeting spaces designed for collaboration:

## Available Meeting Rooms:
- Conference Room A: 12-person capacity, projector, video conferencing
- Conference Room B: 8-person capacity, whiteboard, TV display
- Huddle Rooms (4 available): 4-person capacity each, phone/video capable
- Board Room: 20-person capacity, executive presentations, catering setup
- Phone Booths (6 available): Individual quiet spaces for calls

## Booking Process:
- Reserve through company calendar system (Outlook/Google Calendar)
- Booking window: up to 30 days in advance
- Maximum single booking: 4 hours without manager approval
- Recurring meetings require manager approval for weekly/daily slots
- Cancellations must be made at least 2 hours before meeting start

## Room Equipment:
- All rooms equipped with wireless presentation capability
- Zoom Rooms installed for video conferencing
- Whiteboards and markers available in all meeting rooms
- Power outlets and USB charging stations
- Report equipment issues to facilities@company.com

# Office Supply Management

We maintain well-stocked supply closets for employee convenience:

## Available Supplies:
- Basic office supplies: pens, paper, notebooks, folders
- Computer accessories: cables, adapters, wireless mice
- Presentation materials: flip chart paper, markers, sticky notes
- Kitchen supplies: coffee, tea, snacks, disposable items

## Ordering Process:
- Basic supplies available in supply closets (self-service)
- Special requests submitted through admin team
- Weekly batch orders placed to optimize costs
- Personal items: reasonable use permitted
- Bulk orders for departments require manager approval

# Visitor Management Protocol

We welcome visitors while maintaining security standards:

## Visitor Registration:
- All visitors must be pre-registered by their host employee
- Visitor information required: name, company, purpose of visit
- Valid ID required for building access
- Visitor badges must be worn and visible at all times

## Escort Requirements:
- All visitors must be escorted by an employee
- Hosts responsible for visitor conduct and compliance
- Visitors cannot access secure areas without special authorization
- Maximum visitor duration: 8 hours without special approval

## Visitor Amenities:
- Guest WiFi network available (password: CompanyGuest2024)
- Visitor parking spaces in front lot
- Parking validation available for business visitors
- Guest kitchen access with host supervision

# Building Access and Security

Security measures ensure safe access for all employees:

## Access Cards:
- Key card required for building entry and secured areas
- Business hours access: 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM weekdays
- Weekend access requires security approval
- Lost cards must be reported immediately for deactivation

## After-Hours Access:
- Submit request through security portal
- Manager approval required for weekend access
- Security escort may be required for certain areas
- Sign-in/sign-out required for after-hours access

## Emergency Procedures:
- Fire exits clearly marked on each floor
- Emergency assembly point: front parking lot
- Report emergencies to security: ext. 911 or (555) 123-9911
- First aid stations located on each floor

# Catering and Event Services

We provide catering services for business meetings and events:

## Catering Options:
- Continental breakfast: $12 per person
- Lunch buffet: $18 per person (includes beverages)
- Afternoon snacks: $8 per person
- Coffee service: $3 per person
- Custom menus available for special events

## Ordering Requirements:
- 24-hour advance notice required for all catering
- Minimum order: 10 people for lunch, 15 people for breakfast
- Dietary restrictions accommodated (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free)
- Department cost center required for billing
- Setup and cleanup included in catering price

## Event Planning:
- Conference room setup options: theater, classroom, boardroom
- Audio/visual equipment rental available
- External vendor coordination permitted with approval
- Event planning assistance available through admin team
