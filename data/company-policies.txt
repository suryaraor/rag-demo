# Employee Vacation Policy

Our company provides generous vacation benefits to support work-life balance:

- Full-time employees receive 20 vacation days per year
- Part-time employees receive prorated vacation based on hours worked
- New employees accrue 1.67 days per month in their first year
- Vacation requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance
- Requests are submitted through the HR portal and require manager approval
- Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year
- Carryover days must be used by March 31st of the following year

# Expense Reporting Policy

Our expense reporting process ensures timely reimbursement:

- Monthly expense reports are due by the 5th of the following month
- All receipts must be attached and properly categorized
- Use the company expense management system for submissions
- Manager approval is required before reimbursement processing
- Reimbursements are processed within 5 business days of approval

Travel expense limits:
- Daily meal allowance during business travel: $75
- Hotel expenses over $200/night require pre-approval
- Economy class airfare for domestic travel
- Business class permitted for international flights over 6 hours

# Remote Work Policy

Our flexible work arrangements support productivity:

- Remote work permitted up to 3 days per week
- Manager approval required for remote work arrangements
- Core collaboration hours: 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM (all team members available)
- Standard office hours: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday
- Minimum 2 days in office required per week
- Home office setup: reliable internet and quiet workspace required
- Company provides necessary equipment: laptop, monitor, accessories

# IT Security Requirements

Security is our top priority:

- Passwords must be at least 12 characters with numbers, symbols, and mixed case
- Multi-factor authentication required for all company systems
- Company-provided password manager (1Password) must be used
- VPN required for all remote access to company systems
- Report security incidents within 1 hour to security@company.com
- All devices must have approved antivirus software installed
- Personal devices require MDM enrollment for company access
